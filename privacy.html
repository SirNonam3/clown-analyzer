🤡 Clown Analyzer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 · Applies to the website and the Android and iOS apps.

Short version: there are no accounts. Your games and reviews stay on your device. The engine runs on your device. The free version of the store apps shows ads from Google AdMob, and Premium purchases go through Apple or Google.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Your saved reviews (history), settings, and the usernames you type into the Import tab are stored in your browser or app storage. They are never uploaded to us. You can delete them in the History tab or by clearing the app's data.
  • Game analysis is done by the Stockfish chess engine, which runs on your device.

2. Data sent to third-party services

  • Chess.com and Lichess public APIs: when you import games, the username you enter is sent to Chess.com and/or Lichess to download your public games. (Chess.com privacy, Lichess privacy)
  • Lichess cloud evaluation: during a review, the board positions from the opening phase (no personal data) are sent to Lichess to get deep cached evaluations.
  • Puzzle of the day: fetched from Lichess.

3. Advertising (store apps, free version only)

The free versions of the Android and iOS apps show an ad after a game review is completed, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), your IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later.
  • On iOS, personalised ads are only used if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Premium subscribers see no ads.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (store apps)

Premium subscriptions are paid through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, which receives the store receipt and a random anonymous app user ID (RevenueCat privacy).

5. Website analytics

The website may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free page counting (GoatCounter). It does not track you across sites or collect personal data. The store apps do not use analytics.

6. Children

Clown Analyzer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

7. Your rights and contact

Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled on your device (delete history, clear app data, reset your advertising ID). For any question, open an issue at github.com/SirNonam3/clown-analyzer/issues.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
